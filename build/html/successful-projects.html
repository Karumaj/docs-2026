Successful Projects

This page records projects that met their objectives, delivered within an acceptable time and budget, and were accepted by the client or sponsor. Each entry is classified using the five binary characteristics described on the home page, so patterns behind success become easy to spot.

Note

A project is listed here when it met its approved scope and was formally accepted.
