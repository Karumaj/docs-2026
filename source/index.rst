*About the Repo:* The Repository Contains Key Details of the Completed and Active Ongoing Projects 
Projects can be defined by **five major binary characteristics:**

* Software projects vs. infrastructure projects
* Remotely managed projects vs. onsite-managed projects
* Client-initiated projects vs. internally initiated projects
* End-to-end projects vs. component-based projects
* Full rollout vs. phased rollout
