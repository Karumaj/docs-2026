=======================
Unsuccessful Projects
=======================

This page records projects that were cancelled, significantly over time or
budget, or did not deliver their intended benefits. The purpose is learning,
not blame: each entry notes the likely cause of failure and the lesson carried
forward into future projects.

.. warning::

    Keep entries factual and free of personal blame. Replace the sample rows
    below with your own project data.
